How I work

From the first workshop to UAT sign-off

Step 1

Elicit

Interviews and workshops with buyers, merchandisers, QA and vendors. I find the pain behind the request.

→ Stakeholder map, interview notes
Step 2

Model

As-Is and To-Be process maps, gap analysis, and the business rules that tie them together.

→ BPMN flows, gap matrix
Step 3

Specify

BRD, FRD and user stories with testable acceptance criteria, traced back to business goals.

→ BRD/FRD, backlog, RTM
Step 4

Validate

UAT scenarios, configuration checks and defect triage until the business signs off.

→ UAT plan, test cases, sign-off
