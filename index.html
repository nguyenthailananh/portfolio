Business meets tech.

I started as a developer, so I speak both languages: what the business needs and what engineers can build.

I have seen projects from both sides of the table. I began my career as a .NET developer, and four years of writing code taught me how systems are really built. That experience shapes how I work as a Business Analyst today, helping me turn complex business needs into requirements that are clear, realistic and ready for delivery.

Over the years, I have contributed to products in supply chain, finance, HRM, payments and ticketing, working in Agile teams with clients from the UK, the US, China and the Netherlands, including on-site assignments in Korea and Malaysia. Along the way, I earned the CBAP and Professional Scrum Master I certifications.

Today, I am a Digital Solutions Analyst at Otto International. My next goal is to grow into a Principal Business Analyst or Product Owner role, continuing to bridge business and technology to deliver solutions that create real value.
